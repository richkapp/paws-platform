# Demo assets

The SVG files under `public/illustrations/` are original locally authored demo artwork created for this Paws Platform starter. They are distributed under the root MIT license with attribution to Paws Platform contributors. They depict fictional dogs and a fictional shelter; they are not photographs of real animals or people.

No original shelter photos, logos, third-party stock photos or remote animal-image references are licensed by this document. None are needed for the default demo. The fictional example records in `src/data/demoDogs.ts` are provided under the same MIT terms.

When replacing an illustration with a photograph or another organization's logo, obtain the relevant copyright and privacy/model permissions and record the verified source/license here. Do not infer asset rights from the application-code license. Dependencies have their own upstream licenses.
